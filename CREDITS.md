# Credits

All game content — models, textures, music, ambience, sound effects and voice recordings — is generated by
the scripts in this repository and released under the project's [MIT license](LICENSE).
No third-party asset packs are used.

  [license and third-party notices](https://godotengine.org/license).

## Tools used to generate content (not shipped with the game)

- **[Blender](https://www.blender.org)** 5.1 — GPL-2.0-or-later. Used headless to build and export models;
  the generated models are not covered by Blender's license.
- **[Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)** text-to-speech model by hexgrad — Apache-2.0.
  The voice lines in `assets/voice/` were generated with it (ONNX export from
  [onnx-community](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community)).
- **[kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx)** — MIT. Runs Kokoro on ONNX Runtime.
- **[ONNX Runtime](https://onnxruntime.ai)** — MIT.
- **[eSpeak NG](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng)** (via espeakng_loader and phonemizer) — GPL-3.0.
  Used only at generation time for phonemes; not distributed with the game.
- **[NumPy](https://numpy.org)** — BSD-3-Clause. Music, ambience, sound effects and the "System" voice
  processing are written with it.
